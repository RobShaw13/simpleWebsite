Our Products and Services

Whether you are saving for the future or growing a business, we offer banking services to help you take the next step.

Everyday Checking

Keep your everyday spending simple with an account for purchases, bills, and direct deposits.

Features: No monthly fee, a debit card, and mobile banking.

Personal Savings

Set money aside for a vacation, an emergency, or another goal you are working toward.

Features: $25 opening deposit and automatic transfers from checking.

Business Loans

Give your business room to grow with financing for equipment, expansion, or daily expenses.

Features: Loan options starting at $5,000 and personal guidance from a loan officer.

Home Mortgages

Take the next step toward owning a home with support throughout the application process.

Features: Fixed-rate loan options and guidance for first-time homebuyers.

How It Works

  3. Get Started: Open your account or submit a loan application.
