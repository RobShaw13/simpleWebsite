Our Story

First Reliance Bank opened in Florence, South Carolina, in 1999 with a simple idea: people should be able to ask their banker a question and get a real answer from someone who lives down the road.

Back then, good financial guidance was hard to come by unless you already knew the right person. We set out to change that, one account and one handshake at a time. Today we serve families, homebuyers, and small businesses in Columbia, West Columbia, Lexington, Florence, Greenville, Myrtle Beach, and Mount Pleasant, and we still answer questions the same way.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to open a checking account?

A photo ID, your Social Security number, and an opening deposit. Mention code WELCOME50 for the new customer special.

Can I meet with a banker without an appointment?

Yes, during branch hours. For loans, an appointment with our commercial loan officer lets us give you our full attention.

How does First Reliance keep my information safe?

Our information systems analyst monitors our systems and makes sure only the right people can see your account information.

Do you have an app?

Yes. Online and mobile banking let you check balances, move money, and deposit checks any time.

Is this a real bank?

No. This website and everything on it is a fictional student project.
